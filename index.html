About the sync status pill
Every scan saves to this device instantly, offline or not — nothing waits on a connection. When there's internet, Firestore quietly pushes those scans to your cloud database in the background, and the pill in the header turns green ("Connected"). No wifi at the exact moment of scanning? It stays amber ("Offline — saving on device") and catches up automatically the next time this device gets a signal — no button to press, nothing to remember.
